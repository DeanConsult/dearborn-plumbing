Our Story

Two Decades of Plumbing Excellence in Dearborn MI

Dearborn Plumbing Experts is an association of licensed plumbers with over 20 years of experience in the plumbing industry. Our goal is to always provide the highest level of customer service, and our plumbers have been proudly serving Dearborn for two decades.

All of our plumbers go through rigorous classroom and on-the-job training, plus re-training on the most up-to-date plumbing advancements, so they are prepared to handle any job when they arrive at your home. Our senior plumber has over 25 years of experience as a plumbing contractor.

On February 15, 2021 every plumber in our organization joined a national conference with plumbers around the country to discuss new methods and best practices in plumbing technology. We are always working to bring you the most current methods available.

Our Commitment

Honest Plumbers Who Put Customers First

We understand that most plumbing issues are unexpected. We make it our mission to handle every customer’s issue as if it were our own, and we promise to always complete your job as quickly as possible and make it our top priority.

Our estimates are always at fair market value and competitively priced, so our services never break the bank. We offer no-pressure in-home estimates for any size job, and we can often give estimates over the phone for select services. Every plumber we employ will do their best to service your needs at the lowest cost.

In November 2021 we became a supporting member of Plumbing News and More, one of the main reputable online plumbing publications. We also offer 24-hour emergency service for those times when you need a plumber after hours. We want to be your plumbing company for life.
